# Revision (folding findings and hand edits into a draft)

**Judge the change, not the model.** Every proposed edit is assessed against the brief and the source evidence. Broad rewrites soften supported claims, flatten rhythm, remove framing devices, and insert editorial self-doubt, whichever model produced them. Merge only changes that improve the document while preserving supported claims, voice, quotations, and material qualifications.

**Reframe before cutting.** When a reviewer flags a claim as vulnerable, the reflex is to cut. Ask first: does the strongest defensible version of this claim still make the point? If yes, narrow it. A contestable scaling estimate, for example, can be rescued by stating it as a working heuristic with the variables named, which forces a hostile reader to argue about orders of magnitude rather than decimals, and that is the terrain the argument wins on.

**Patch before rewrite.** Ninety percent of the time the issue is one paragraph. Use your editor tool's exact-string replace for word swaps, paragraph replacements, and line rewrites (low drift); rewrite the whole file only when the change is structurally deep and distributed across most of the document.

**Copy before patching a versioned document.** `cp v2.md v3.md`, then edit v3; on a synced folder, copy then delete rather than moving. The prior version stays for side-by-side comparison and for the adversarial re-read.

**Editorial notes stay out of the body.** "This needs harder data in the next draft," "this should be stated modestly," and their kin are notes, not argument; they live in a marked notes section or a comment. Material evidentiary qualifications stay beside the claims they limit; moving them out of view makes the body misleading.

**Hard constraints survive every pass.** Legal lines, locked wording, and confidentiality rules from the frame are re-read verbatim after each patch round.

**Triage order.** Read all findings once before touching the draft. Apply the single highest-leverage fix (usually the tell), re-read, then the rest in priority order, stopping at nitpicks. Convergent findings first; a lone flag is adjudicated and, when rejected, gets one line saying why. Address the evidence gaps the claim depends on; defer the nice-to-haves.

**Operating data lands last.** Structural cuts and prose passes first; then inject the operator's numbers into paragraphs the structure has prepared to receive them. A draft missing its operating data is also more legible to a cold reader, who will name the absence.

**The owner's hand edit is ground truth.** When the owner rewrites a passage, the rewrite stands; fix dictation spelling silently, and where their compression rounds off a nuance, say so and let them decide how much precision to restore. Keep the pair (draft, edit) as a voice sample.
