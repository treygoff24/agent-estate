# One-pager (leave-behind, internal skim, overview)

**Reader.** Someone who will give it ninety seconds: a contact after a meeting, an executive between calls, a decision-maker deciding whether to read the long version.

**Spine.** Explainer or ask. Page 1 holds everything: a bottom-line paragraph, a small grid of the hard numbers, dates, and identifiers, a short body of titled sections, and at most one boxed "what it opens" callout. Page 2, if any, is numbered sources only. Cut content rather than spill onto a second page.

**Register.** This is the licensed exception to the prose default: compression is the genre, so tight bullets, fact grids, and bold lead-ins are correct. Everything else holds: organizations glossed inline, the bottom line free of jargon, no performance, honest softening.

**Outreach one-pagers.** The one-pager is what gets attached to a cold email ("I've attached a one-pager for a quick overview"); the long document never is. Evidence is evidence, not the ask.

**Review floor.** Readers: one persona matched to the room it is going into (a friendly reader for a leave-behind, a hostile one for anything that could be forwarded), plus lint.
