# Follow-up bump (reply on an existing thread)

**Reader.** Someone who already has the pitch in the thread below and needs one new fact: the window.

**Spine.** Ask. Two to four sentences, zero new pitch content. The only payload is the new time window, plus optional itinerary proof ("I'll be in town next week"). Never narrate that this is a follow-up or a last try; the recipient knows. A decline needs the fact and the counter-offer, not the reasoning.

**Register.** Warm text-message cadence: contractions, one exclamation allowed, a plain sign-off with no title block. A warm revival of a dead thread names the person, then one sentence on what has moved since, then the feedback ask.

**Review floor.** Lint only.

**Worked example:**

> Hi Sam,
>
> Following up here, as I'll be in town next week. Do you have time to connect before noon next Tuesday, the 16th? I'd love your thoughts and feedback on the draft proposal as we work toward a pilot.
>
> Best,
> Alex

Second form, a last-minute bump before travel: open on the window itself ("I've got a window tomorrow after 3:30 if that works for you"), or with a soft human opener ("Just wanted to see if you're available before I head out").
